What each problem is

  1. parser: the shared parser read dates month first, and every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so 7 April came out as 4 July.
  2. hint: booking-v3 printed MM/DD/YYYY under the date. Day-first people typed the month first as told, and the form read it day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants.
  3. one-day: the desk read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). Day and month are right, but the date is a day off, and in one report it differs between Lisbon and Toronto. A swap can’t do that, so the parser fix doesn’t touch it. Likely a time-zone or date-boundary bug.
