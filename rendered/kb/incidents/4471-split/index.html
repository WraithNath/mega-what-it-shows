How each report was placed

  1. hint: the report came in through the booking form, and the tenant runs booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date, so day-first people type the month first, and the form then reads it day first. The parser fix does not touch this: the hint has to change.
  2. one-day: the desk read the body as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). Day and month are right; the date is a day early or late, and in one report differs between Lisbon and Toronto. A swap can’t do that. It is a time-zone or date-boundary bug.
  3. parser: everything else from a day-first tenant. The shared parser reads the date month first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 describes.

Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, so it isn’t used here. The tenant directory decides.
